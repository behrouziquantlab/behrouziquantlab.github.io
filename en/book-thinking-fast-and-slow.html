Thinking, Fast and Slow

Thinking, Fast and Slow

by Daniel Kahneman

Find the book ↗

Key ideas

  • System 1 is fast and intuitive; System 2 is slow and effortful, and lazy.
  • Losses loom larger than equal gains (loss aversion).
  • We judge by what we see and ignore what is missing ("what you see is all there is").
  • Overconfidence survives because feedback is noisy.

Our critique

Not a trading book, and more important than most that are. Some priming studies it cites have failed to replicate; the core findings on loss aversion and overconfidence remain robust and directly relevant to traders.

Trading involves substantial risk. BQL tools analyse markets and send alerts; they never place trades. In our own published tests divergence alone showed no statistical edge — use it as one input of your own process.
