Key ideas

  • The big money is made by sitting tight in the right trend, not by frequent trading.
  • Hope and fear are the trader's real enemies.
  • Losing money is part of learning; repeating the same loss is not.

Our critique

Timeless on temperament, dated on mechanics. Read it for psychology and humility — Livermore himself went bankrupt several times.
