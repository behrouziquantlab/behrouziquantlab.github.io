Signal lifecycle

  1. FORMING

    First closed bar after a candidate swing that did not extend it, with the divergence relation already present.

  2. CONFIRMED

    Swing confirmed by the engine rule and matched to a MACD pivot; the relation still holds. Never repaints.

  3. INVALIDATED

    The swing extended or the final reading was not the same divergence. The forming label disappears.

Confirmation delay, disclosed

Median delay from pivot to confirmation: about 2 bars (fast engine) and about 12 bars (standard engine). The exact delay is printed in each label tooltip.
