04 · SIGNAL LIFECYCLE

Every signal has a state. Every state has a timestamp.

Divergence can only be confirmed after price moves away from a swing. Instead of hiding that delay, the indicator shows each stage and labels it.

  1. FORMINGSeen one closed bar after a candidate swing that did not extend. Drawn faint, with "?" in the label.
  2. CONFIRMEDThe engine rule confirmed the swing and the relation still holds. Final — never moved or deleted.
  3. INVALIDATEDThe swing extended, or the final reading was a different relation. The forming label is removed.

Reading a label

HD▲M5·S

  • HDType: HD hidden, RD regular
  • ▲Direction: ▲ bullish, ▼ bearish
  • M5Timeframe: M5 or M1
  • SEngine: S standard 12,26,9, F fast 3,5,2

Confirmation delay, disclosed

Median delay from pivot to confirmation: about 2 bars for the fast engine and about 12 bars for the standard engine. The exact value is printed in every label tooltip.

INSTALLATION

Running in five minutes.

  1. In MetaTrader 5 open File → Open Data Folder.
  2. Copy BQL_Divergence_Intelligence.ex5 to MQL5\Indicators\BQL\.
  3. Restart MT5, or right-click Navigator → Refresh.
  4. Drag BQL_Divergence_Intelligence onto an M1 or M5 chart.
  5. For phone alerts, enable push in Tools → Options → Notifications.

Your licence file goes to the Common\Files folder as BQL_licence.txt — or paste the key into the "Licence key" input. The full manual is delivered with the release.

08 · QUESTIONS

Before you buy.

Does it repaint?

Confirmed signals never repaint and are never removed. Forming signals are marked with "?" and disappear if they are invalidated — that is their purpose.

Does it trade for me?

No. It analyses and alerts. It never opens, closes or modifies orders.

Will it make me profitable?

No indicator does that by itself. Our own 12-month study found divergence alone reached 1R about 50% of the time. Use it as one input to a process you already trust.

Which markets and timeframes?

Any symbol in your MT5 terminal. Engines run on M5 and M1 in release 1.0.

How is the licence delivered?

After your payment is confirmed you receive the installer, the manual and a key bound to your MT5 account number — usually within one business day.
