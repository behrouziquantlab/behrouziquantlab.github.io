Key ideas

  • There is no single right method — but every great trader controls risk.
  • Successful traders fit a method to their personality.
  • Patience in waiting for the right trade is a recurring theme.

Our critique

Inspiring and varied, but survivorship bias is built in: we only hear from those who won. Use it for principles, not for strategies.
