Emotional control under real money

You cannot switch emotions off. You can design your trading so they have less to act on.

Emotions follow uncertainty

Stress peaks when the outcome is open and the stake feels large. Reduce both: smaller size makes each outcome tolerable; pre-defined exits remove decisions from the hot moment.

A practical protocol

  • Before: size the position so the stop loss is an amount you can accept calmly.
  • During: no new analysis after entry; the plan was made when you were calm.
  • After: a two-line journal entry — what happened, and did I follow the plan?

Tilt

After a loss, the urge to "win it back" is strong and predictable. Treat a fixed number of consecutive losses as a hard stop for the day.

Sources & further reading
