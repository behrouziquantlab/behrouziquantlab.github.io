Discipline is a system, not a personality trait

Traders who "lack discipline" usually lack rules that are specific enough to follow.

Contents

Vague rules failMore trading, worse resultsBuild the system

Vague rules fail

"Don't over-trade" is not a rule. "Maximum three trades per session, and none after two losses" is. Discipline becomes easy when the rule is concrete, measurable and decided in advance.

More trading, worse results

Barber and Odean studied tens of thousands of retail accounts: the households that traded most earned the lowest net returns. Activity feels like progress; costs and impulsive decisions say otherwise.

Build the system

  1. A written checklist for every setup.
  2. A daily loss limit enforced by stopping, not by willpower.
  3. A weekly review: rules followed? rules broken? why?
Note Automation is the strongest form of discipline. Alerts that fire only when every condition is met remove the temptation to "see" a setup that is not there.
