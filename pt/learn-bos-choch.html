Break of structure and change of character

BOS continues a trend; CHoCH is the first sign it may be ending. Precise definitions remove the guesswork.

In this lesson

DefinitionsClose, not touchThe protected lowPractise

Definitions

  • BOS (break of structure): in an up-trend, a candle closes above the last confirmed swing high. The trend continues.
  • CHoCH (change of character): in an up-trend, a candle closes below the swing low that produced the last higher high. The up-trend's logic is broken.

Close, not touch

A wick through a level is a probe, not a break. Requiring a candle close removes most false breaks — at the cost of a later signal.

The protected low

After a BOS in an up-trend, the swing low that launched the breakout becomes the protected low. As long as it holds, pull-backs are corrections; once it breaks on a close, you are in a new regime.

Practise

On M5, mark every BOS and the protected low after it. Count how often a CHoCH was followed by a real trend change, and how often price resumed the old trend.
