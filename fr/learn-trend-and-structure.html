Trend and market structure

Highs and lows are the skeleton of every chart. Learn to read them before anything else.

Swings define the trend

An up-trend is a sequence of higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL); a down-trend of lower highs (LH) and lower lows (LL). When neither sequence holds, the market is ranging.

Which swings count?

Every wiggle is not a swing. A practical rule: a swing is confirmed only after price moves away from it by a meaningful amount — for example a multiple of the Average True Range (ATR). This adds a delay, but it removes noise.

Moving averages as a summary

EMA 20 above EMA 50, with price above both, summarises an up-trend. It is a description, not a signal: averages lag by construction.

Practise

Mark the last six confirmed swings on an M15 chart. Write HH/HL/LH/LL next to each and decide: trend up, trend down, or range?
