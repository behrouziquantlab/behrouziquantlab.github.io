One market, three lenses

A practical professional stack: M15 for context (trend, key zones), M5 for the setup (structure, divergence), M1 for timing (the candle that confirms entry). Each lower timeframe must agree with the one above it.

Rules that prevent most mistakes

  1. Never take a lower-timeframe signal against the higher-timeframe trend without a reason you can write down.
  2. Decide on closed candles of the timeframe you are using.
  3. A higher-timeframe bar is only "closed" after its own period ends — not when the M1 bar inside it closes.

Why this matters for indicators

Many indicators silently use the current, unfinished higher-timeframe bar. That creates signals that look perfect in history and fail live. Ask any vendor how they align timeframes; we document ours.
