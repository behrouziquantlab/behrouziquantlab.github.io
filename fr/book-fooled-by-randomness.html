Fooled by Randomness

by Nassim Nicholas Taleb

Find the book ↗

Key ideas

  • Judge decisions by the range of outcomes that could have happened, not only the one that did.
  • Survivorship bias makes winning strategies look safer than they are.
  • Rare, extreme events dominate long-run results.

Our critique

Provocative and repetitive, but the central lesson — separate skill from luck before trusting a result — is the foundation of honest backtesting.
