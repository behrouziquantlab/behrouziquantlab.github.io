The FX market in numbers: who trades $7.5 trillion a day

The largest market in the world is also the most decentralised. What that means for a retail trader.

Size and concentration

The Bank for International Settlements' 2022 survey measured average FX turnover at about $7.5 trillion per day, concentrated in a few financial centres led by London and New York, and dominated by the US dollar on one side of most trades.

No single exchange

FX trades over the counter. Your broker's price is one quote among many; spreads, liquidity and execution quality vary by provider and by session. That is why signals should be judged on closed bars and with costs included.

The retail reality

European regulators require CFD providers to disclose the share of retail accounts that lose money; at the time of the 2018 intervention ESMA reported loss rates typically between 74% and 89%. Any tool that promises otherwise deserves suspicion.
