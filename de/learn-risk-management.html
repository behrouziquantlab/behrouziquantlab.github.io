Risk management and position sizing

The only part of trading fully under your control. Size from the stop, not from conviction.

In this lesson

Risk per tradeExpectancyDrawdown arithmeticRules worth writing down

Risk per trade

Choose a fixed fraction of equity to risk per trade — professionals commonly use 0.25%–1%. Position size = (equity × risk %) ÷ (stop distance × value per pip).

Expectancy

Expectancy per trade = win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss. With a 3R target you only need more than 25% winners to break even before costs; with 1R you need more than 50%. Always include spread and commission when you evaluate a system.

Drawdown arithmetic

A 20% drawdown needs a 25% gain to recover; 50% needs 100%. Small, consistent risk keeps you in the game long enough for an edge to show.

Rules worth writing down

  • Maximum daily loss — stop trading for the day when it is hit.
  • No widening of stops after entry.
  • A written reason for every trade, reviewed weekly.
