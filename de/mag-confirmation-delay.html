Every swing has a delay — measure it, do not hide it

A pivot can only be confirmed after price moves away from it. We show you exactly how long that takes.

A swing high is only a swing high once price has moved away from it. Any indicator that marks the exact top instantly is either using future bars or redrawing its history.

In our standard engine a swing is confirmed after a pull-back of 4 × ATR. The median delay from pivot to confirmation is about 12 M5 bars; the fast engine needs about 2 bars.

We tested smaller swing sizes. A 3 × ATR swing confirms 4 bars earlier, but loses signals and does not improve quality. We kept 4 × ATR and instead show forming signals one bar after the pivot, clearly marked with "?" so they are never mistaken for confirmed ones.

Every label in BQL Divergence Intelligence states when the pivot formed and when it was confirmed. Transparency about delay is what makes a non-repainting signal trustworthy.
