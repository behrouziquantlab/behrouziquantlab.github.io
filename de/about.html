01 · OUR STANDARD

Three rules every BQL tool follows.

01

Deterministic

The same bars always produce the same signal. No discretion, no hidden randomness, no recalculated history.

02

Verified

Engines are regression-tested bar by bar against an independent reference before release. Version 1.0 matched on every one of 34,451 events.

03

Disclosed

Delays, limitations and negative results are published. Our own research found that divergence alone has no statistical edge — and the product page says so.

WHAT WE WILL NEVER PUBLISH

Trust is built by what you refuse to say.

  • Ratings or reviews that do not exist
  • User numbers we cannot prove
  • Guaranteed profit or win-rate promises
  • Comparisons with products we have not tested
