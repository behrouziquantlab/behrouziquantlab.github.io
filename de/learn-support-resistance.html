Support and resistance as zones

Levels are areas where orders cluster — not lines that price must respect to the pip.

Contents

Why zones, not lines

Support and resistance appear where many participants acted before: previous swing highs and lows, round numbers, the open of the session. Orders cluster around a price, so think in zones.

Strength of a zone

  • More reactions = more participants remember the area.
  • A clean, fast rejection says more than a slow drift.
  • Each new test consumes orders; the fourth test breaks more often than the first.

Role reversal

Broken resistance often becomes support on the retest — because traders who missed the break place orders there.

Practise

Find three zones on your chart where price reacted at least three times. Mark the width of each zone using the wicks, not the bodies.
