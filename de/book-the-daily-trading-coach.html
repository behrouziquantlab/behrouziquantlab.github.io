Key ideas

  • Treat trading like performance: deliberate practice, feedback, review.
  • Build on strengths rather than only fixing weaknesses.
  • Keep a structured journal of decisions and emotions.

Our critique

Highly practical and modular — ideal to read one lesson a day. Less useful if you do not already trade with a defined process.
