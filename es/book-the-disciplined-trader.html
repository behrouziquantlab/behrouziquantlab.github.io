The Disciplined Trader

by Mark Douglas

Find the book ↗

Key ideas

  • Markets offer unlimited freedom; without self-imposed rules that freedom becomes destructive.
  • Painful memories distort perception of new opportunities.
  • Discipline is the bridge between knowing and doing.

Our critique

Dense and more philosophical than his later book. Valuable for traders who keep breaking their own rules; less practical for beginners.

El trading conlleva un riesgo considerable. Las herramientas BQL analizan los mercados y envían alertas; nunca abren operaciones. En nuestras pruebas publicadas, la divergencia por sí sola no mostró ventaja estadística: úsela como una pieza de su propio proceso.
