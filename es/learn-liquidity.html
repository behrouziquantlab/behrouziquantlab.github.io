Liquidity: where the orders are

Stops above highs and below lows are liquidity. Sweeps happen because large orders need it.

In this lesson

What liquidity means on a chart

Every obvious swing high has buy-stops above it (short sellers' stops, breakout buyers). Every obvious low has sell-stops below. These clusters are liquidity: orders a large participant can trade against.

Sweep and reclaim

A sweep is a quick move beyond a high or low that triggers those stops, followed by a return inside the range — a reclaim. A sweep alone proves nothing; the reclaim on a closed candle is the information.

Equal highs and lows

Two or more swings at nearly the same price attract stops on the same side, making them attractive targets. Treat "double tops" as liquidity first, pattern second.

Note Liquidity concepts describe where price may travel. They do not tell you when. Combine them with structure and a confirmed trigger.

Practise

Mark equal highs/lows on M5 for one week. Record how often they were swept, and how often the sweep was reclaimed within three candles.
