Key ideas

  • Getting wealthy and staying wealthy require different skills.

  • Your personal experience of markets shapes your risk tolerance more than data does.

Our critique

Elegant and accessible; aimed at investors rather than short-term traders. Its emphasis on margin of safety translates directly to position sizing.
